Download **Triiv.gba** and open it in any GBA emulator (mGBA, SkyEmu, Delta, RetroArch) or on a flash cart.

Arcade rework: the game is the Gauntlet. START, pick a ship, fly. No callsign, no Campaign or Free Play. Waves 1-2 are easy drones, hunters carry no names, big HIGH SCORES page, one-lesson HOW TO PLAY, smoother cloud lightning.
